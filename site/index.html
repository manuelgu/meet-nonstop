      What this is and isn't

      • Destinations, not schedules. A route marked seasonal has no dates attached, and a once-weekly summer charter looks the same here as a five-a-day trunk route. Check a real booking site before you commit to anything.
      • Nonstop, not direct. A “direct” flight can still touch down somewhere under the same flight number. Everything here is genuinely nonstop.
      • Community data. Routes come from the airline-and-destination tables on English Wikipedia, matched to airports through redirect resolution and Wikidata IATA codes. Large airports are well maintained; small ones are patchier. Routes confirmed by both endpoints' articles are marked as such.
      • Fairest sorts by the longest single leg anyone has to fly, so nobody eats the whole journey. Shortest total minimises combined distance, which is better for carbon and worse for whoever lives furthest out.
